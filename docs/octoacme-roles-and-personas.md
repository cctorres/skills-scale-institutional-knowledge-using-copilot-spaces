# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Release Managers

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate release readiness and deployment so changes can be delivered safely and with clear communication.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate release readiness, deployment windows, and required release evidence
- Ensure rollback or mitigation plans are documented and ready
- Coordinate stakeholder announcements and release retrospectives

### Interactions
- Work with Developers on release candidates and rollback readiness
- Coordinate with the QA Lead on test and acceptance evidence
- Align timing and communication with the Project Manager (PM)
- Confirm release scope and expected outcomes with the Product Manager
- Share release status and announcements with Stakeholders

---

## QA Leads

### Role Summary
QA Leads guide the team's test strategy and quality practices, helping ensure that work meets acceptance criteria and is ready for release.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy, quality gates, and acceptance evidence
- Coordinate test coverage and defect triage
- Identify and manage quality risks

### Interactions
- Collaborate with Developers on testability, coverage, and defect fixes
- Align test planning with the PM's capacity and milestones
- Clarify acceptance criteria with the Product Manager
- Coordinate release-readiness evidence with the Release Manager

---

## UX/Design Leads

### Role Summary
UX/Design Leads shape usable, accessible experiences and maintain design standards across the project.

### Responsibilities
- Translate customer needs into user flows, prototypes, and designs
- Own user experience and design standards, including accessibility considerations
- Validate designs and experience through appropriate research or testing

### Interactions
- Partner with the Product Manager on customer needs and outcomes
- Work with Developers on feasibility and implementation
- Coordinate usability acceptance with the QA Lead
- Align design activities, planning, and dependencies with the PM

---

## Business Analysts

### Role Summary
Business Analysts clarify business needs and requirements, making them actionable and traceable through delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Clarify requirements and document process impacts
- Identify stakeholder needs and open questions
- Maintain traceability from intended outcomes and requirements to backlog items

### Interactions
- Connect Stakeholders and the Product Manager to the delivery team
- Support Developers and the QA Lead with clear, testable requirements
- Help the PM maintain plans, assumptions, and dependencies

---

## Security Leads

### Role Summary
Security Leads guide security requirements and risk management throughout delivery and release.

### Responsibilities
- Define security requirements and review threat and compliance risks
- Advise on secure implementation and security testing
- Provide security incident guidance and support
- Review security risks, mitigations, and release controls

### Interactions
- Advise Developers on security controls and implementation choices
- Coordinate with the QA Lead on security testing and evidence
- Work with the PM on security risks and mitigations
- Help the Product Manager assess security priorities and trade-offs
- Coordinate deployment safeguards with the Release Manager

---

## Data/Analytics Leads

### Role Summary
Data/Analytics Leads help teams define how success will be measured and use reliable data to understand outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define success metrics, instrumentation requirements, and reporting approaches
- Advise on data quality and validate measurement plans
- Analyze outcomes and share findings to inform decisions

### Interactions
- Align measurable goals with the Product Manager and PM
- Advise Developers on instrumentation and telemetry
- Support the QA Lead with data and analytics validation
- Communicate outcome findings to Stakeholders

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
